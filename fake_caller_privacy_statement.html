Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you use certain features of the App, you may provide information that includes:

  • Contact Information: If you choose to pick a contact, the App may access the selected contact's name and photo so you can customize the fake call experience.

  • Custom Settings: The App stores settings you choose, such as caller name, selected images, ringtone choice, timing preferences, and related customization options.
  • Share Actions: If you use the share feature, the content you choose to share is passed through Apple's standard share sheet and the destination you select.

We use the information we collect to:
