Privacy Policy for aBop

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to aBop! This Privacy Policy explains how Hot Potato Apps ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use the aBop mobile application and its iMessage extension (the "App"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Information Stored on Your Device

aBop stores the information needed to provide its features on your device, including Nudge and Surprise details, notification preferences, and app settings. This information is used to present, schedule, update, and remove the experiences you create. You can remove this information by deleting the relevant item in the App or uninstalling the App.

Messages Content

aBop is designed for use in Apple Messages. The content you choose to send, including Nudge, Surprise, Crossfire, and DRAW messages, is shared with the recipients you select through Apple's Messages service. We do not operate a service that stores your Messages conversation history or your contacts.

DRAW Content and Secure Device Credentials

When you use the cloud option to judge a DRAW game, the prompt and the drawings submitted for that game are sent to our Google Cloud Run service and then to OpenAI for the limited purpose of selecting a winner and generating commentary. The service does not store the drawings or prompt after processing. It records only limited security and operational information needed to protect the service, including App Attest challenges, public-key and credential records, replay counters, request identifiers, device-attestation metadata, and service usage information such as timing and token counts.

aBop uses Apple App Attest to help protect the DRAW judging service from unauthorized use. App Attest creates a device-specific credential; we store a protected representation of that credential and related verification information, not your Messages content.

Information Collected Automatically

Our service providers may process limited technical information needed to operate the App and its services, such as IP address, device and browser metadata, operating system version, request timing, and error information. We use this information to provide, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App.

Information You Provide

You may provide content such as Nudge or Surprise text, DRAW prompts and drawings, and Crossfire game input. That content is used to provide the feature you selected and, when you send it through Messages, is shared with the people in the conversation.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App and its iMessage experiences.
  • Deliver the features you choose, including Nudges, Surprises, Crossfire games, and DRAW judging.
  • Schedule local notifications or alarms when you enable them.
  • Protect the DRAW service, prevent fraud and abuse, and investigate technical problems.
  • Operate Game Center features and process eligible in-app purchases.
  • Deliver and measure advertising in Crossfire Blast where available.
  • Respond to your comments and questions and comply with legal obligations.

Third-Party Services

Apple Messages, Notifications, and App Attest

Apple Messages delivers the messages you choose to send. Apple's notification and AlarmKit services deliver local reminders that you enable. Apple App Attest is used to verify eligible devices before they may initiate secure DRAW requests. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for information about how Apple processes data through these services.

OpenAI and Google Cloud

We use Google Cloud Run to operate the secure DRAW judging service and OpenAI to analyze submitted DRAW prompts and drawings and return a winner and commentary. These providers process the information described in the DRAW Content and Secure Device Credentials section only to provide the service to us. For more information, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy and the OpenAI Privacy Policy.

Apple Game Center

Crossfire Blast uses Apple Game Center for player authentication, invitations, matchmaking, multiplayer play, and related game features. Game Center information is handled by Apple to provide those features. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

Google AdMob

Crossfire Blast may use Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob may process device information and app usage information to deliver ads, support measurement, and prevent fraud. Where required, the App presents Google's consent flow before requesting ads. Please refer to the Google Privacy Policy for more information.

Apple In-App Purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases. We receive purchase-status information needed to provide or restore an entitlement, but we do not receive or store your payment card or other financial details. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for more information.

Data Retention

Content stored locally remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the App. DRAW images and prompts processed by our cloud judge are not retained by our service after the judgment is completed. We retain limited security and operational records only as long as reasonably necessary to protect the service, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our agreements.

Data Security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards designed to protect the information we process. DRAW cloud access is protected with device-specific Apple App Attest credentials. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing. You can also manage App notification permissions through your device settings, remove local App content, and choose not to use cloud DRAW judging.

To exercise applicable rights or ask a privacy question, contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

aBop is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to consent to the processing of personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner requiring parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us so we can address the request.
